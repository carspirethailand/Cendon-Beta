About us

Every car is trying to tell you something.

SpireONE is the assistant that listens on your behalf — and remembers what it heard.

Why we exist

A car communicates constantly. A new vibration at 80 km/h. A note in the engine that was not there last month. A service sticker that expired while you were busy. None of this is hidden — it is simply spread across time, and no one is keeping the record.

Most people are not careless about their cars. They are outnumbered by the detail. Between the odometer, the fluid intervals, the tyre dates, the last three quotations from three different garages and the receipt that went through the wash, the information exists but never assembles into a picture. By the time it does, it usually costs money.

SpireONE exists to assemble that picture and keep it assembled.

What we build

SpireONE is a car-care assistant built around a single idea: the owner should not have to become an expert to be treated fairly. The product does four things.

  • It keeps the record. Mileage, service history, parts fitted, costs paid. Entered once, remembered permanently, and readable in one screen rather than a folder of paper.
  • It watches the clock. Intervals are tracked against how far you actually drive, not against a date you have to remember. When something is due, you hear about it before it is overdue.
  • It answers questions in plain language. Describe a symptom, record a sound, photograph a warning light or upload a quotation. You get an explanation written for an owner, not for a technician.
  • It shows you what parts should cost. Prices are researched centrally per model and shared, so you walk into the conversation already knowing the range.

How we work

Plain language is not a feature

An explanation that a driver cannot act on is not an explanation. We write for the person holding the keys, in the language they speak. Every part of SpireONE works in Thai and in English.

Nothing you did not ask for

We do not sell your data, and we do not build a second business out of it. Our incentives should be legible from the pricing page alone. What we collect and why is set out in full in our Privacy Policy.

An estimate is labelled an estimate

Parts of SpireONE are powered by AI, and AI is confident even when it is wrong. We show our workings: where a figure came from, how recent it is, and how much confidence sits behind it. When we do not know, the product says so rather than inventing a number.

You can leave with your data

Your service history belongs to you. It can be exported at any time, and deleting your account deletes it. A record you cannot take with you is not really yours.

Who we are

SpireONE is built and operated by Car Spire Thailand, an independent team based in Thailand. We are not owned by a manufacturer, a dealership group, a parts distributor or an insurer, and we do not accept payment to rank one garage above another.

We are a small team. That shows in how quickly we answer, and occasionally in how long a feature takes.

Where we are going

The record is the foundation. Everything we build next stands on it: earlier warnings from the data you have already given us, honest price ranges for more of the market, and a service history that means something when the car changes hands.

เราสร้างอะไร

SpireONE คือผู้ช่วยดูแลรถที่สร้างขึ้นบนความคิดเดียว — เจ้าของรถไม่ควรต้อง กลายเป็นช่างก่อน ถึงจะได้รับการปฏิบัติอย่างเป็นธรรม ตัวผลิตภัณฑ์ทำสี่อย่าง

  • จดบันทึกให้ เลขไมล์ ประวัติการเข้าศูนย์ อะไหล่ที่เปลี่ยน ค่าใช้จ่ายที่จ่ายไป กรอกครั้งเดียว จำถาวร และอ่านได้ในหน้าจอเดียว ไม่ใช่ในแฟ้มเอกสาร
  • นับระยะให้ รอบบำรุงรักษาถูกนับตามระยะที่คุณขับจริง ไม่ใช่ตามวันที่ที่ต้องจำเอง ถึงกำหนดเมื่อไหร่จะรู้ก่อนที่จะเลยกำหนด
  • ตอบคำถามด้วยภาษาคน เล่าอาการ อัดเสียง ถ่ายรูปไฟเตือน หรืออัปโหลดใบเสนอราคา แล้วได้คำอธิบายที่เขียนให้เจ้าของรถอ่าน ไม่ใช่เขียนให้ช่างอ่าน
  • บอกว่าอะไหล่ควรราคาเท่าไหร่ ราคาถูกสำรวจไว้กลาง แยกตามรุ่นรถและใช้ร่วมกัน คุณจึงเดินเข้าไปคุยโดยรู้ช่วงราคาอยู่แล้ว
