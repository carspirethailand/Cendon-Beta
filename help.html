Support

Get help

Start with the answers below. If none of them fit, write to us — a person reads every message.

Something feels wrong with the car right now

If you notice a fault in the brakes, steering, tyres, fuel system or electrics, stop driving and have the vehicle inspected. SpireONE is not a substitute for a qualified technician, and it should never be the reason you keep driving.

Account

I cannot sign in

Use the same method you signed up with — an account created with Google will not open with a password. If you used email, use “Forgot your password?” on the sign-in screen. If the page never loads, your browser may be blocking third-party sign-in windows; allow pop-ups for this site and try again.

How do I export my data?

Profile → Account → Export. You get your vehicles, service history and costs in a file you can keep.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Account → Delete account. This removes your vehicles, records, uploads and chat history. Backups are overwritten within 30 days. It cannot be undone, so export first if you want a copy.

Vehicles and reminders

A reminder came at the wrong time

Reminders are driven by mileage, not by the calendar. If your odometer reading is out of date the estimate drifts. Open the car, update the mileage, and the schedule recalculates immediately.

The mileage moves on its own

That is intentional. Between the readings you enter, SpireONE estimates distance from how far you normally drive, so a reminder does not wait for you to open the app. Every reading you enter corrects the estimate.

I am not getting notifications

Check that notifications are allowed for this site in your browser or phone settings, and that the channel is still connected under Profile → Settings. On iPhone, notifications only work if you have added SpireONE to the Home Screen.

Prices and parts

A price looks wrong

Prices are estimates researched per vehicle model and refreshed weekly. They are a starting range for a conversation, not a quotation. Genuine, OEM and aftermarket parts differ widely, and labour is not included. If a figure is clearly wrong, report it and we will correct the record for everyone.

Why do store links go to a search page?

Because a search result stays valid. Direct product links break when a seller removes a listing, and a dead link is worse than a search that finds the current equivalent.

The assistant

How accurate is it?

Treat it as a well-read second opinion, not a diagnosis. It is good at explaining what a symptom usually means, what to check first, and whether a quotation looks reasonable. It cannot see, hear or touch your car.

I have run out of quota

Quotas reset daily. Your remaining quota is shown under Profile → Usage. Paid plans raise the limit.

The app itself

I am still seeing an old version

The app updates itself, but a browser can hold on to a cached copy. Reload the page while holding Shift (or Ctrl+Shift+R). On a phone, close the app completely and reopen it.

Does it work offline?

Your saved vehicles and records stay readable without a connection. Anything that needs the assistant, prices or news needs the network.

Still stuck?

Write to carspirethailand@gmail.com. We are a small team, which means a real person answers — usually within two business days.

บัญชี

เข้าสู่ระบบไม่ได้

ให้ใช้วิธีเดียวกับตอนสมัคร บัญชีที่สร้างด้วย Google จะเปิดด้วยรหัสผ่านไม่ได้ ถ้าสมัครด้วยอีเมล ให้กด “ลืมรหัสผ่าน?” ที่หน้าเข้าสู่ระบบ ถ้าหน้าต่างไม่ขึ้นเลย เบราว์เซอร์อาจกำลังบล็อกหน้าต่างล็อกอินอยู่ ให้อนุญาต pop-up สำหรับเว็บนี้แล้วลองใหม่

ดาวน์โหลดข้อมูลของตัวเองอย่างไร

โปรไฟล์ → บัญชี → ดาวน์โหลดข้อมูล จะได้ข้อมูลรถ ประวัติการบำรุงรักษา และค่าใช้จ่าย เป็นไฟล์ที่เก็บไว้เองได้

ลบบัญชีอย่างไร

โปรไฟล์ → บัญชี → ลบบัญชี จะลบข้อมูลรถ บันทึก ไฟล์ที่อัปโหลด และประวัติการสนทนา ข้อมูลสำรองถูกเขียนทับภายใน 30 วัน การลบย้อนกลับไม่ได้ ถ้าต้องการสำเนาให้ดาวน์โหลดออกไปก่อน
